Features

Sab kuch jo school ko chahiye

Ek simple platform — tracking se lekar safety tak. School, drivers aur parents, sab ek page par.

Live GPS tracking

Bus ki real-time location map par dekho — ETA ke saath. Parents ko har second pata rahega bus kahan hai.

Attendance

Driver har stop par ek tap mein attendance mark karta hai. Boarded ya absent — record automatic save ho jaata hai.

Child-safety alerts

Agar koi bachcha board nahi hua ya drop nahi hua, school aur parents ko turant alert milta hai. Trip safely complete hone tak nazar.

No app install needed

App store ke chakkar nahi. Bas link kholo aur track karo. Parents ka phone storage free, no updates, no hassle.

Works on any phone browser

Android ho ya iPhone, naya ho ya purana — koi bhi browser mein chalta hai. Low data, fast loading.

Affordable for Indian schools

Mehngi hardware ya costly GPS device ki zaroorat nahi. Budget-friendly pricing jo har school afford kar sake.

How it works

Teen simple steps

Setup mein minutes lagte hain. Na hardware, na training — bas shuru karo.

  1. 1

    School sets up routes

    Admin routes aur stops banata hai, drivers aur buses assign karta hai. Sab kuch ek dashboard se.

  2. 2

    Parents join with a code

    School ka join code share karo. Parents khud register karte hain — bachche ko link karo, ho gaya.

  3. 3

    Track the bus live

    Bus chalti hai, parents live location dekhte hain aur safety alerts paate hain. Tension free.

Built for everyone

Har role ke liye banaya gaya

Parents

Bus live track karo, board/drop notifications pao, aur mann ki shanti. Bachcha safe pahuncha — confirm.

Drivers

Bade buttons, simple screen. Trip start karo, attendance lo, drop karo — sab kuch ek tap mein, driving ke beech.

Schools

Poori fleet ek dashboard se manage karo — routes, drivers, students, aur safety records. Parents ka bharosa badhao.
